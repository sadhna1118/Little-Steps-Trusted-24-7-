# Little Steps – User & Operations Guide
## 24×7 Childcare Platform User Manual

---

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Little Steps**, the digital childcare platform built for 24×7 working parents, daycare centers, and safety auditors. This guide provides step-by-step operational instructions for all three platform personas.

---

## 2. Guide for Parents 👤

### 2.1. Searching & Filtering Daycares
1. On the homepage, select your city (e.g., **Bengaluru**, **Mumbai**, **Pune**, **Delhi-NCR**, **Hyderabad**).
2. Select your child's age group (**Infant**, **Toddler**, **Preschool**, or **After School**).
3. Toggle quick filters:
   - `🌙 24x7 Round-the-Clock`
   - `⭐ Night Shift Care (6 PM - 8 AM)`
   - `🚨 Emergency Drop-in Support`
4. Adjust the **Hourly Budget Slider** on the left sidebar to match your budget.
5. Click **"View & Book ➔"** on any center card to open the detailed profile.

### 2.2. Exploring Daycare Details & Safety Standards
- **Photo Tour:** View facility photos, toddler play zones, and soundproof sleep pods.
- **Caregiver Roster:** Review caregiver photos, years of experience, pediatric nursing degrees, and police verification badge numbers (`POL-BLR-XXXX`).
- **Safety Features:** Verify CCTV streaming availability, HEPA filtration, and emergency hospital tie-ups.
- **Official Accreditations:** Check state daycare licenses, Fire NOCs, and ISO certifications.

### 2.3. Booking a Childcare Slot
1. Click **"⚡ Book Childcare Slot"**.
2. Select your plan: **Hourly Flexi**, **🌙 Night Care**, **Full Day**, or **Monthly Membership**.
3. Fill in your child's name, age group, dietary/allergy instructions, and emergency contact.
4. Set the date and start time.
5. Review the live fee breakdown and click **"Confirm & Send Request ➔"**.

### 2.4. Live Child Activity Monitoring
- Switch to the **Parent Dashboard** to view your active checked-in child banner.
- Monitor timestamped milestone logs in real time:
  - 🥣 Meals & Snacks consumed
  - 😴 Sleep pod naps logged
  - 🎨 Sensory play & activities
  - 🌡️ Temperature & health logs
- Click **"🎫 View QR Pass"** to display your high-security biometric check-in/out barcode.

## 3. Guide for Daycare Providers 🏢

### 3.1. Provider Command Center
1. Click the **"🏢 Provider"** pill in the top navigation bar.
2. Monitor center metrics:
   - **Currently Checked-In Children**
   - **Real-Time Capacity Utilization Rate** (e.g., 22 / 35 Spots Filled)
   - **Pending Booking Requests**
   - **Gross Bookings Revenue**

### 3.2. Managing Booking Requests
- In the **Booking Requests & Reservations** table:
  - Click **"✓ Accept"** to confirm an incoming reservation and lock in the slot.
  - Click **"✕ Decline"** to cancel a request if capacity is full.

### 3.3. Digital Check-In & Live Milestone Updates
1. When a child arrives, click **"📥 Check In Child"** to update their status and automatically alert the parent.
2. During their shift, click **"+ Post Update"** or **"📝 Post Log"**:
   - Choose the event type (Meal, Nap, Learning, Health Check).
   - Enter details (e.g., *"Aarav consumed warm dinner and is asleep in Pod 4"*).
   - Click **"Broadcast to Parent App"**.
3. When the parent arrives for pickup, click **"✓ Check Out"** to conclude the session.

### 3.4. Staff & Pricing Management
- Click **"+ Onboard Caregiver"** to add certified nurses and assign day/night rotations.
- Click **"⚙️ Center Settings & Rates"** to update capacity limits and hourly/night tariffs.

---

## 4. Guide for Safety Admins 🛡️

### 4.1. Daycare Verification & Document Inspection
1. Click the **"🛡️ Admin"** pill in the top navigation bar.
2. In the **Daycare Certification Queue**:
   - Review submitted center applications (e.g., *Bloomfield Montessori*).
   - Click **"🔍 Inspect Docs"** to inspect uploaded State Operating Permits, Fire Safety NOCs, and Police Background clearances.
   - Click **"✓ Approve Certification"** to grant the official **"Little Steps 24×7 Certified"** trust badge.

### 4.2. Caregiver Safety Registry Audit
- Inspect the central registry of all caregivers across all daycares.
- Verify background check status tags and CPR certification validity dates.

### 4.3. Dispute Resolution & Platform KPIs
- Track parent inquiries and dispute resolutions.
- Monitor platform KPIs: Total Registered Parents, Verified Centers Ratio, Average Utilization Rate, and Total Gross Bookings.
